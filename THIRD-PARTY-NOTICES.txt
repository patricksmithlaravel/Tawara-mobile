THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
===================

The Android and iOS applications built from this repository include the
third-party software listed below. Each entry names the crates that are under
a licence and gives the text of that licence as the crates publish it, with the
copyright lines it carries.

Each crate is listed with its version, its repository and the authors its
metadata names, so that it can be traced to the people who wrote it. The
authors are not necessarily the copyright holders. The holders are the ones
the licence text names.

Most texts come from the crate's package. Where the package has no licence
file, or keeps it under a name the generator does not read, the text is taken
from that file or from the crate's repository, at the commit the package was
published from. Some crates publish their licence with no copyright line, in
the package and in the repository alike; their text is given as they publish
it.

Where a crate carries no text of its licence, the licence's standard text is
given, and the entry says so. A few crates under the MIT licence publish no
text of it anywhere; for them the standard text is given without the template
line it leaves for the copyright holder.

The entry for tawara-app under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 covers the fonts
the application bundles.

The Mochimo Cryptocurrency Engine License Agreement, version 1.0, applies to the
application's own crates and to the mochimo-crypto library. It has no SPDX
identifier, so the generator does not print its text. The text is reproduced
in full at the end of this file, from LICENSE.md.

The graph described here is the one for the Android builds (arm64-v8a and
x86_64) and the iOS device build.
